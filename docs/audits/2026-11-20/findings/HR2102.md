## CORE findings (headline, plainEnglish, howItWorksToday, changes)

0 MISSTATES, 5 MINOR.

### C1. changes row 5 "Cost to the federal government" — CONDITION DROPPED ("as estimated by", "approximately") — MINOR
- Summary: "Direct federal spending rises by $13 billion from 2027 through 2036. Spending that depends on future funding
  decisions rises by $7 billion from 2027 through 2036."
- Source (FLOOR, CBO table inserted by Rep. Bost, "By fiscal year, millions of dollars"): "Direct Spending
  (Outlays).............. 800 5,750 12,750" and "Spending Subject to Appropriation 0 3,100 7,450 (Outlays)".
- Fault: $12.75 billion and $7.45 billion are given as "$13 billion" and "$7 billion" with no "about", and as a flat
  statement of what happens rather than a CBO estimate. The same source lists "Areas of significant uncertainty". Bost's
  own spoken figure is "a combined $20 billion in total spending". (The elided CBO prose may itself round to $13 billion
  and $7 billion; not checkable.)

### C2. plainEnglish — CONDITION DROPPED ("approximately") — MINOR
- Summary: "some supporters said its $13 billion added federal spending from 2027 through 2036 should be paid for."
- Source (FLOOR, CBO table): direct spending 2027-2036 "12,750" (millions). Rep. Bost: "passing this bill would cost a
  combined $20 billion in total spending completely unpaid for." Rep. Rogers: "I just believe we should pay for it."
- Fault: rounded figure with no "about"; and the figure the supporter actually spoke was $20 billion combined, not
  $13 billion. The "some supporters" credit itself is right (Rogers: "reluctant support"; Bost: "I rise today in support").

### C3. changes row 4 "When the change starts" (ifPassed) — EXACT WORD CHANGED — MINOR
- Summary: "It applies only to payments for months after that date."
- Source (CITED Sec. 2(c)): "shall take effect on the first day of the first month beginning after the date of the
  enactment of this Act and shall apply to payments for months beginning on or after such date."
- Fault: "on or after" became "after". Read literally, the summary leaves out the first month, which begins on that date.
  Same slip, lighter, in whatItDoes 2 ("for months after the law starts").

### C4. changes row 2 "The cap on the separate combat payment" (today) — EXACT WORD CHANGED (looser) — MINOR
- Summary: "the separate combat payment is capped by a service-based formula."
- Source (USC 10 §1413a(b)(3)(B)): "the amount of the payment under paragraph (1) for any month may not, when combined
  with the amount of retired pay payable to the retiree after any such reduction ... cause the total of such combined
  payment to exceed the amount equal to the retired pay percentage ... of the member's years of creditable service
  multiplied by the member's retired pay base".
- Fault: the law caps the combined total (combat payment plus the retirement pay left after the reduction), not the
  combat payment on its own. Practical meaning is close.

### C5. headline, changes rows 1-3 (and details.question, whatItDoes 1, whoItAffects 1) — EXACT WORD CHANGED — MINOR
- Summary: "combat-injured military retirees" / "combat-injured person" / "Combat-injured retirees".
- Source (USC 10 §1413a(e)): "combat-related disability" means a disability that "(1) is attributable to an injury for
  which the member was awarded the Purple Heart; or (2) was incurred ... (A) as a direct result of armed conflict; (B)
  while engaged in hazardous service; (C) in the performance of duty under conditions simulating war; or (D) through an
  instrumentality of war." Sec. 2: "eligible combat-related disabled uniformed services retiree". CBO (FLOOR):
  "qualifying combat-related disabilities".
- Fault: "combat-injured" is narrower than the legal term, which covers hazardous service, training that simulates war
  and instruments of war. Mitigation: members use "combat-injured" on the floor (Houlahan, Goodlander, Ruiz), plainEnglish
  itself says "combat-related disabilities", and whatItDoes 1 adds "who qualify for the separate combat payment".

## EXTENDED findings

### E1. unknowns 2 — ABOUT THE SOURCES — MISSTATES (borderline)
- Summary: "The public cost estimate is described in the record, but the separate listed estimates do not provide amounts."
- Source (SOURCE CBO): "2026-10-27: H.R. 2102, Major Richard Star Act. As modified by H. Res. 1247"; (FLOOR, Bost): "I
  include in the Record the CBO report. H.R. 2102, MAJOR RICHARD STAR ACT, AS MODIFIED BY H. RES. 1247 ... Direct Spending
  (Outlays).............. 800 5,750 12,750".
- Fault: this is a remark about what the pipeline fetched, not about the bill. The listed 27 Oct 2026 estimate carries the
  same title as the report printed in the Record with full amounts, so a reader is told the CBO estimates give no amounts
  when the same summary quotes those amounts three times. If judged only as source-talk, it is MINOR.

### E2. unknowns 1 — ABOUT THE SOURCES — MINOR

- Source (STATUS): "Received in the Senate and Read twice and referred to the Committee on Armed Services."
- Fault: source-talk wording. The underlying point (no Senate vote is scheduled in the record) is fair.

### E3. whatItDoes 3 — NO SOURCE (inference) — MINOR
- Summary: "It does not repay earlier reductions."
- Source (Sec. 2(c)): "shall apply to payments for months beginning on or after such date."
- Fault: the section does not say this in terms; it is a fair reading, and the packet's own warning flags the line.

### E4. whatChangesForYou — NO SOURCE (invented example) / CONDITION DROPPED — MINOR
- Summary: "A soldier medically retired after 12 years because of a combat injury could keep both checks in full."
- Source: no source gives a 12-year example. Sec. 2 covers "an eligible combat-related disabled uniformed services
  retiree, who is retired under chapter 61 of this title with fewer than 20 years of creditable service"; §1413a(a) and (d)
  make eligibility depend on electing benefits and applying under Defense Department procedures.
- Fault: illustrative example not in any source; "soldier" for all uniformed services; the eligibility step is left off
  ("could" softens it).

### E5. whatChangesForYou — NO SOURCE — MINOR
- Summary: "People outside this group would not see their own pay change."
- Source: none states this. Sec. 2(b)(3) also amends section 1414 ("Conforming amendments reflecting end of concurrent
  receipt phase-in period"); the packet does not say what effect, if any, those have on others.

### E6. whoItAffects 3 — CONDITION DROPPED ("approximately") — MINOR
- Summary: "The budget office estimates $13 billion more in direct federal spending from 2027 through 2036."
- Source (FLOOR, CBO table): "12,750" (millions). Credit to the budget office is right; "about" is missing.
- Also: "Taxpayers" as an affected group is the summary's own label; no source uses it (nearest is Bost: "we are just
  making regular Americans' day-to-day lives more expensive").

### E7. caseFor 1 (Houlahan) — WRONG CREDIT (slight) — MINOR
