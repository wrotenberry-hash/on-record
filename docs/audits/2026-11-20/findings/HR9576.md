## CORE findings

### C1. headline — WRONG CREDIT / CONDITION DROPPED — MINOR
- Summary: "Creates a permanent federal unit to prosecute fraud against taxpayer-funded programs."
- Source (Sec. 2): "There is hereby established a National Fraud Enforcement Division within the Department of Justice under the authority of the Attorney General"; "(c) Duties.--The Assistant Attorney General for National Fraud Enforcement shall perform such duties as the Attorney General may prescribe."
- Source (CRS): "This bill provides statutory authority for the National Fraud Enforcement Division ... The division investigates and prosecutes fraud against taxpayer dollars and taxpayer-funded programs. The National Fraud Enforcement Division was established by DOJ in April 2026."
- Source (FLOOR, Van Drew): "ensuring this powerful shield for the American taxpayer becomes a permanent fixture of our justice system."
- Why: "permanent" is a supporter's word (Van Drew), not the bill's or CRS's; they say "established" / "statutory authority". The fraud-prosecution purpose is CRS's description of the existing division; the bill text itself assigns no fraud duty (the summary's own changes row 3 says so). "Creates" is supported by "hereby established", though the unit already exists. Same overall meaning for a lay reader, so MINOR.

### C2. plainEnglish and changes row 2 (ifPassed) — WRONG CREDIT (credited to Sec. 2) — MINOR
- Summary (plainEnglish): "led by a presidentially chosen and Senate-confirmed official"
- Summary (changes row 2, source "Sec. 2"): "It would be led by an Assistant Attorney General chosen by the President and confirmed by the Senate."
- Source (Sec. 2): "The Division shall be headed by an Assistant Attorney General appointed under section 506, and designated by the President as the Assistant Attorney General for National Fraud Enforcement."
- Source (FLOOR, Van Drew): "the division will permanently be led by an Assistant Attorney General appointed by the President, whomever that is, and confirmed by the Senate"
- Why: Sec. 2 does not say "Senate" or "chosen by the President"; it says "appointed under section 506" and "designated by the President". 28 U.S.C. 506 is not in the packet. The only packet text stating Senate confirmation is Rep. Van Drew's floor statement. The statement is given as plain fact and credited to Sec. 2. Likely the same meaning, but it cannot be confirmed from the cited section alone.

### C3. howItWorksToday item 1 — NO SOURCE — MINOR

- Source: no packet source defines the Department of Justice or says this. CRS says only "the National Fraud Enforcement Division within the Department of Justice (DOJ)".
- Why: background definition not found in any source and credited to CRS, which does not contain it.

## EXTENDED findings

### E1. whatChangesForYou, first sentence — NO SOURCE — MINOR (the most serious extended item)
- Summary: "A person reporting suspected fraud in a taxpayer-funded program could see cases handled by a permanent Justice Department division."
- Source: no packet source mentions members of the public reporting fraud, how reports are routed, or any effect on a person who reports. CRS says only "The division investigates and prosecutes fraud against taxpayer dollars and taxpayer-funded programs."
- Why: an invented example / prediction. It also suggests a change for the reader although the division already exists and already handles these cases (CRS: "established by DOJ in April 2026"). Hedged with "could", so rated MINOR.
- Second sentence ("The bill does not change benefits, taxes, or reporting rules for ordinary people.") is consistent with the full bill text: Sec. 2 contains only the establishment, the head, the duties clause, a clerical amendment and the political-activity amendment.

- Why: same issue as C2. Senate confirmation appears in the packet only in Van Drew's floor statement; section 506 is not in the packet.

### E3. whoItAffects item 1 — NO SOURCE — MINOR
- Summary (section "Sec. 2"): "Justice Department fraud investigators and prosecutors. Their office would be written into federal law instead of only created by the department."
