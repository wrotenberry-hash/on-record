- Summary: "The budget office estimates $1 million more automatic spending, $1 million less revenue, and $1 million in spending needing yearly approval."
- Source (FLOOR, CBO estimate): "CBO estimates that enacting H.R. 8278 would increase direct spending by $1 million, decrease revenues by $1 million, and increase the deficit by $2 million over the 2026-2036 period.
